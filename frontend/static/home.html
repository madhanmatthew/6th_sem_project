The Problem & Our Solution

The Problem

  • Ambulances choose hospitals manually — often not the closest or best-equipped
  • No automated traffic signal clearance along emergency routes
  • Hospital ICU/bed data is unavailable at dispatch time
  • No real-time coordination between ambulance, police, and hospital teams
  • Average emergency response time in Bengaluru: 18–26 minutes

Our AI Solution

  • ML model scores 20 real Bengaluru hospitals on distance, beds, trauma capability
  • ETA regressor predicts arrival time and selects optimal ambulance from 12 real bases
  • Traffic agent simulates green corridor — signals synchronized along the route
  • Multi-agent coordinator resolves conflicts between ambulance, police and hospital agents
  • AI system ETA: 10–17 minutes — saving 8–12 minutes per emergency
What We Built (MVP)
🧠

Hospital AI Engine

RandomForest Q-value model scores 20 real Bengaluru emergency hospitals on free beds, distance, trauma capability, and specialty match.

🚑

Ambulance ETA Model

GradientBoostingRegressor predicts arrival time using distance, traffic level, and weather. Selects from 12 real BBMP EMS bases across 21 Bengaluru locations.

🚦

Traffic Signal Control

RandomForest predicts congestion and simulates green corridor activation — signals synchronized across all junctions on the route.

🚓

Police Coordination

Severity classifier deploys police units and sets perimeter radius. TF-IDF + LogisticRegression on emergency text reports.

⚖️

Multi-Agent Coordinator

Detects conflicts between agent decisions (e.g. route vs perimeter), runs bid negotiation, and produces a unified dispatch plan.

🗺️

Live Map Dashboard

Leaflet + OpenStreetMap shows incident pin, ambulance animation, hospital selection, and green route corridor in real time.
